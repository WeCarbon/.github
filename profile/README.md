# WeCarbon

WeCarbon is a global ClimateTech firm offering AI-powered sustainability solutions. Leveraging innovative technology and global thought leadership, it empowers infrastructure & logistics, testing & certification, and global trade to enhance operational efficiency, reduce carbon emissions, and accelerate green and AI transitions.

🌐 [we-carbon.com](https://www.we-carbon.com)

## Open source

- [Noddit](https://github.com/WeCarbon/noddit): one place for what your AI agents are doing and what they need you to decide — markdown files in git, a desktop app and a terminal bench. `npm i -g @wecarbon/noddit`
- [formist-cli](https://github.com/WeCarbon/formist-cli): `fm`, the command-line client for [Formist](https://formist.ai). Spaces, knowledge-base search, chats and files from your terminal. `pipx install formist-cli`
